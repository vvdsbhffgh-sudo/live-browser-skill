## Why Atlas

Atlas — أطلس — the map-bearer. The agent carries the map: sitemap, tabs,
devices, models, agents, modes — one tool holding the whole world it can
reach. It also says the safety promise in a different way: the world is
mapped and visible, nothing hidden.
