How this works

The AI drives a real Chromium browser through the Atlas MCP server. Every action it takes appears above, and the screenshot refreshes after each step.

Google sign-in is manual, once, by you — with a dedicated Google account, never your main personal account. Never paste a password in chat or give it to the AI.

This skill never pays. Checkout and payment pages are blocked, and card numbers are never typed.

Pause stops the agent so you can take over the browser yourself; Resume lets it continue. Record starts/stops saving the session (action log + screenshots) to live/ on this machine only — recording starts and stops only when you click it.

Endpoints: /status · /latest.png · /events (SSE) · MCP: /mcp
